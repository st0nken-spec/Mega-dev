## Current state

The product brief (`PRODUCT-BRIEF.md`) defines the vision and locked decisions. The app has shipped past the walking skeleton: separate child profiles plus Play (matching, snake, tic-tac-toe, two runner games), Create (coloring, drawing, tracing) and Homework hubs, as an installable PWA. The architecture and self-hosting documents still describe a proposed, reversible default - the app currently stores progress in browser local storage with no server side.
