# Backlog research: jungle runner and panther revenge

## Identification

Ivan's childhood memory closely matches **Disney's Tarzan (1999)** by Eurocom for PlayStation/PC/N64. Contemporary descriptions call it a 2.5D platformer with coins/tokens, jumping, tree surfing and action stages. The Stampede stage runs toward the camera ahead of elephants; the separate `Sabor Attacks` level is the leopard boss fight. The animal is Sabor, described in the game as a leopard, rather than a panther.

The exact memory may combine two parts: the quick run/jump/collect feel from the side-scrolling, tree-surfing and Stampede segments, then the Sabor fight. That is the right emotional target, but Mega-dev should not copy Disney characters, names, art, music, level layouts or dialogue.

## Original game loop to preserve safely

- immediate run, jump and duck controls;
- readable obstacles and a quick restart;
- tokens along risky and safe paths;
- short levels with changing pace;
- a final predator boss whose attacks can be learned;
- satisfying second-chance difficulty - Ivan always lost and wants his revenge.

## Mega-dev version

Build an original **Jungle Run** with a new child explorer/animal hero and an original big-cat guardian. Use short 60-90 second stages. Collect learning tokens, jump/duck obstacles and finish with a forgiving pattern-based boss.

Learning should fit movement rather than pause it:

- collect only the requested number, letter, word or category;
- choose the lane that completes a simple sum or Swedish word;
- spoken prompts for children who cannot read yet;
- boss shield opens after a correct, age-scaled choice;
- optional pure-fun mode with no questions.

Use generous checkpoints, visual attack tells, adjustable speed and no lost lives. Parent-selected difficulty can scale from symbols/counting through Swedish/English vocabulary and general knowledge.

## Roadmap placement

Research/prototype after M1's matching-game skeleton. A one-level input/performance spike can enter M3, but the polished runner and boss should remain backlog until the shared profile, content and progress systems are stable.
