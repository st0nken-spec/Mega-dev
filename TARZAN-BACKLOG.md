# Backlog research: jungle runner and panther revenge

## Identification

Ivan's childhood memory closely matches **Disney's Tarzan (1999)** by Eurocom for PlayStation/PC/N64. Contemporary descriptions call it a 2.5D platformer with coins/tokens, jumping, tree surfing and action stages. The Stampede stage runs toward the camera ahead of elephants; the separate `Sabor Attacks` level is the leopard boss fight. The animal is Sabor, described in the game as a leopard, rather than a panther.

The exact memory may combine two parts: the quick run/jump/collect feel from the side-scrolling, tree-surfing and Stampede segments, then the Sabor fight. That is the right emotional target, but Mega-dev should not copy Disney characters, names, art, music, level layouts or dialogue.

## Original game loop to preserve safely

- immediate run, jump and duck controls;
- readable obstacles and a quick restart;
- tokens along risky and safe paths;
- short levels with changing pace;
- a final predator boss whose attacks can be learned;
- satisfying second-chance difficulty - Ivan always lost and wants his revenge.

## Mega-dev version

Build an original **Jungle Run** with a new child explorer/animal hero and an original big-cat guardian. Use short 60-90 second stages. Collect learning tokens, jump/duck obstacles and finish with a forgiving pattern-based boss.

Learning should fit movement rather than pause it:

- collect only the requested number, letter, word or category;
- choose the lane that completes a simple sum or Swedish word;
- spoken prompts for children who cannot read yet;
- boss shield opens after a correct, age-scaled choice;
- optional pure-fun mode with no questions.

Use generous checkpoints, visual attack tells, adjustable speed and no lost lives. Parent-selected difficulty can scale from symbols/counting through Swedish/English vocabulary and general knowledge.

## Roadmap placement

Research/prototype after M1's matching-game skeleton. A one-level input/performance spike can enter M3, but the polished runner and boss should remain backlog until the shared profile, content and progress systems are stable.

# Backlog research: hero training platformer

## Identification

Ivan's memory closely matches **Disney's Hercules / Hercules Action Game (1997)** by Eurocom for PlayStation and Windows. It is a 2D side-scrolling action platformer with pseudo-3D foreground/background lanes plus over-the-shoulder running stages. Hercules runs, jumps, grabs ledges, swings on poles, fights, and collects coins, action figures, sword upgrades and hidden password vases.

## Original feel to preserve safely

- fast run-and-jump movement with short, varied stages;
- satisfying item trails and hidden routes;
- occasional foreground/background lane changes;
- simple environmental interactions and readable boss patterns;
- over-the-shoulder obstacle runs as a pacing change.

Mega-dev must use original characters, art, audio, names and level designs. Build an original **Hero Training** game: a child hero crosses a myth-inspired obstacle course, collects stars/runes, solves brief choices through movement, and faces friendly pattern-based trials.

Natural learning angles:

- number sequences and sums determine the correct item trail;
- Swedish/English word parts sit on alternate lanes;
- shapes, categories and general-knowledge symbols hide on optional routes;
- level-end hero badges show effort and skill, not failure or lost lives;
- pure-fun mode remains available.

## Roadmap placement

Prototype one short stage after M1. Consider it for M3 alongside Jungle Run once shared controls, profile/progress data and curriculum-tagged content packs are stable.

# More revival candidates - not committed scope

These are options for Ivan to pick from after the first shared game system works:

- **Crate Dash** - a Crash Bandicoot-style toward-camera runner with dodging and crate smashing; trains reaction/timing and counting or grouping crates.
- **Ring Rush** - a Sonic-style speed platformer with ring trails; practices counting and quick arithmetic from collected-ring totals.
- **Market Rooftops** - an Aladdin-style 1993 platformer with gem routes; basic addition/subtraction and value comparison.
- **Letter Glider** - Rayman-style floating and precision jumps; collect letters in order to spell Swedish or English words.
- **Animal Kingdom Trails** - Jungle Book / Lion King 1994-style nature levels; animal facts, habitats and general nature knowledge.

These describe broad gameplay patterns only. Use original characters, worlds, art, audio and level designs.
